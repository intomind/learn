Middle school

Labs

Five labs, each run on a demo in Chrome or Edge, with an IntoMind One on your head.

Lab 1Alpha FlowerMeasure what your brain does when you close your eyes, and grow a fractal from it.Lab 2BCI Flight SimulatorSteer a ship by looking at flickering lights, read from the back of your brain.Lab 3Age DetectorAn AI model on the IntoMind One estimates your age from your EEG. How close does it get?Lab 4Mind-Controlled Light SwitchTeach a model two of your own thoughts, test it fairly, and switch a light with it.Lab 5Mind-Controlled MicrocontrollerYour custom AI head switches a light on a small board, with no computer in the room.

High school

Labs

Five labs, four on the demos and the last on a board you program, each with an IntoMind One on your head.

Lab 1Alpha FlowerMeasure relative alpha power, and find out what else changes it, including where the device sits.Lab 2BCI Flight SimulatorSteer by looking at flickering lights: how the visual cortex follows a flicker, and how a decoder reads it.Lab 3Age DetectorAn AI model estimates your age from your EEG. Measure its error, and compare it with a simpler guess.Lab 4Mind-Controlled Light SwitchChoose two mental states and a place on the head, teach a model to tell them apart, and test it fairly.Lab 5Mind-Controlled MicrocontrollerYour custom AI head switches a board's pin, with no computer in the loop.

College

Labs

Six labs, five on the demos and the last on a board you program. Each is a lab manual: the background with its math, a hypothesis before any data, the procedure, an analysis in code, and a report.

Lab 1Alpha FlowerRelative alpha power by Welch's method, eyes open against closed at Oz and Fpz, tested by permuting the blocks.Lab 2BCI Flight SimulatorFrequency tagging, the demo's score rebuilt in code, and the information rate a faster decision buys.Lab 3Age DetectorTest the head's any-site claim on one person by permuting blocks of windows, and its error against the room's most common age.Lab 4Biological Sex DetectorWhat a score means when two groups overlap far more than they differ.Lab 5Mind-Controlled Light SwitchTwo mental states and a site, a ridge head scored on held-out rounds, a permutation test over rounds, and the mirror site.Lab 6Mind-Controlled MicrocontrollerA MicroPython program closes the loop on a board's pin, and ten timed calls test a model of the delay.

Graduate

Labs

Six labs, five on the demos and the last on a board you program. Each says exactly what its demo computes, asks for a preregistration before any data, and ends in analyses in your own code, open problems, and a report.

Lab 1Alpha FlowerIndividual alpha frequency two ways, reactivity, and alpha above the background, with their stability within a person and between sites.Lab 2BCI Flight SimulatorThe demo's decoder rebuilt, canonical correlation and bits a minute against the window, and the half-field test at O1 and O2.Lab 3Age DetectorThe age head's bias toward its training mean and its scatter in a room of similar age, two sites compared with an equivalence test.Lab 4Biological Sex DetectorWhat an AUC near 0.6 implies and where averaging stops, confounds simulated and adjusted, and a design that could tell brain from contact.Lab 5Mind-Controlled Light SwitchTwo sessions on two days: each head tested against its relabelings with two penalties, the transfer raw and recentered, and probes for what it learned.Lab 6Mind-Controlled MicrocontrollerThe loop timed on the board's own clock against the window model, false switches at rest with their bounds, and the board's prefix tested.
